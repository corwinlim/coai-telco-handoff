# Build 12 — Customer Discovery & Pilot Decision

Status: **INTERVIEWS NOT STARTED / EVIDENCE NOT COLLECTED**. This document is a plan, not proof of customer demand.

## Hypothesis to falsify
Frontline telecom support staff and dispatchers lose measurable time or context when transferring connectivity complaints to technicians, and a structured handoff brief is meaningfully better than their current ticketing/CRM templates.

## Roles and sample
Seek **3 independent conversations**: frontline support agent, dispatch/field technician, and support lead/operations manager. Target actual role experience; record employer only if permission is obtained. Do not imply that anyone has been contacted.

## Data safety
Use synthetic SIM-101 through SIM-110 only. **Do not ask for customer phone numbers, locations, account IDs, screenshots of live CRM, call recordings, credentials, or sensitive work files.** Ask for process descriptions and approximate non-confidential timings. Do not publish respondent names without explicit permission.

## 20-minute discovery guide (neutral, no pitching first)
1. Walk me through the most recent handoff you personally handled, without customer details.
2. What must be known before a technician can act? Which fields are mandatory?
3. Where is that information captured today (CRM, ticket template, chat, spreadsheet)?
4. What was missing last time, and what happened as a result?
5. How often does re-contact, clarification, or a repeat dispatch occur? Is there a log or report that could verify the frequency?
6. What is the approximate prep time per handoff? How do you measure it?
7. Would a separate app create duplicate entry or compliance problems?
8. Who owns process change and could sponsor a limited pilot?
9. If given a synthetic SIM-101 demo, what would you change or reject?
10. What evidence would convince your team to trial this for two weeks?

## Prototype comparison (only after interviews)
Give each participant the same **fictional** incident and compare existing workflow vs CoAi handoff:
- Count missing mandatory fields with a participant-approved checklist
- Capture start/end time for each method
- Ask whether any suggested next step overstates certainty
- Note extra entry work, failures, rejection reasons and preferred integration
- Use matched fictional cases or counterbalance order to limit practice effects
- Avoid presenting findings as proof of network diagnosis or real customer outcomes

## Red-team investment gate
| Why Continue | Stop / Kill Criteria |
|---|---|
| 3 roles independently describe meaningful handoff omissions | Fewer than 2 of 3 report recurring pain |
| One buyer can describe a lawful, consented pilot path | No process owner or no permitted synthetic pilot |
| Measurable time or field-completeness improvement | Extra duplicate data entry offsets any benefit |
| Fits current ticketing governance | Requires prohibited access to customer data or impersonation |

**GO (only for a limited synthetic pilot):** 3 genuine interviews logged; at least 2 show recurring, material pain; one process owner permits a small, non-sensitive evaluation; and no safety/compliance blocker. **NO-GO for live telecom data, integration, production sales or claims of proven revenue** until a separate permission, security and procurement review.
