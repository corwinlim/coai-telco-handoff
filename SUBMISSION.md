# Submission — CoAi Telco Handoff

**Who is it for?** Telecom support agents preparing technician handoffs.

**What problem?** Incomplete, scattered and unverified connectivity complaints cause repeat troubleshooting and unclear technician preparation.

**What was built?** A working single-page synthetic incident-intake-to-handoff workflow. Choose or edit a fictional incident, generate a structured brief, inspect missing information and unverified claims, acknowledge review, then download or copy the technician brief. Inputs edited after review revoke export permission until the brief is regenerated and reviewed.

**How to use:** Open the deployed site; choose SIM-101; click Generate handoff brief; review reported facts and missing data; tick acknowledgement; click Download .txt.

**Limitations:** Synthetic examples only, deterministic processing, no real coverage verification, no telecom provider integration, no identity-based operator approval, and no real customer data. Not affiliated with any carrier.

**Working demo URL:** https://coai-telco-handoff.vercel.app/
